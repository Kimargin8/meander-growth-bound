## Prior work

The connectivity transfer follows the frontier method of Iwan Jensen. The previous published lower bound used for comparison is due to Michael H. Albert and Michael S. Paterson. The papers describe further sources and distinguish their contributions from the calculations in this release. The companion paper also cites Efthimios Alepis for the separate tree/bitmap enumeration and known terms. [Jensen](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/33/34/301) · [Albert and Paterson](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2005.02.006) · [Alepis](https://github.com/talepis/meander-tree-bitmap).
